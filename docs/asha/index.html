45-Minute Office Module (chair-friendly)

Part 1 · ~8 min

Opening & Loosening

  • Invocation: Om Sahana Vavathu
  • Hands in-out and hands-stretch breathing
  • Hands and trunk twisting
  • Neck movements, shoulder rotations

Part 2 · ~18 min

Yogasana

  • Ardha Kati Chakrasana
  • Vakrasana, seated Sashankasana
  • Ardha Chakrasana, Padahastasana
  • Paschimottanasana, Ardha Matsyendrasana
  • Trikonasana

Part 3 · ~19 min

Relaxation & Pranayama

  • Seated Deep Relaxation Technique (DRT)
  • Abdominal, thoracic, clavicular and full yogic breathing with mudras
  • Nadi Shuddhi, Bhramari
  • Closing prayer and silence

Safety Guidelines

  • Practise on an empty stomach (2–3 hours after a meal).
  • Wear loose clothing; practise barefoot on a mat or beside a chair.
  • Move within your limits. Do not force a stretch or hold your breath under strain.
  • Tell the instructor beforehand about surgery, injury, high blood pressure, heart conditions or pregnancy.
  • Keep silence during relaxation and pranayama.
