Corporate Yoga & Wellness Workshop

Long hours at a desk cause neck, shoulder and back strain, mental tiredness and stress. This 60-minute workshop combines gentle movement, stretching, breathwork and deep relaxation. No special equipment or room needed.

Benefits

  • Neck, shoulder and back relief: eases tension from long sitting.
  • Stress and anxiety management: regulated breathing helps calm the nervous system.
  • Focus and energy: restores clarity for the rest of the workday.
  • Easy to organise: no change of clothes, works in a meeting room or online.

For Event Organisers

  • Space: conference room, open office area, or a virtual broadcast. Office chairs or yoga mats.
  • Attire: comfortable work clothes are fine. Practise on an empty stomach or at least 2 hours after a light meal.
  • Inclusive: every practice has easier options for joint sensitivity and all fitness levels.
  • Audio: for hybrid or large groups, arrange a clear microphone for guided relaxation and breathwork.
