Rooted in Patanjali, trained in the S-VYASA tradition

Smile Yoga is not a fitness studio. Every class follows the classical approach of Patanjali's Yoga Sutras (posture, breath, relaxation and meditation together), as taught at S-VYASA, Bangalore, where Asha completed her YIC and M.Sc.

How each posture is taught

  1. Name and demonstrate the pose
  2. Explain benefits and who should avoid it
  3. Guide step by step with the breath
  4. Hold, observe and gently correct
  5. Release, relax, then questions

Deskasana · Corporate Workshop

Deskasana: 60 minutes to reset the workday

Long hours at a desk cause neck, shoulder and back strain, tiredness and stress. This workshop combines gentle movement, stretching, breathwork and deep relaxation. No special equipment or room needed.

Neck & back reliefEases tension from long sitting
Less stressRegulated breathing calms the nervous system
More focusClarity and energy for the rest of the day
Easy to runMeeting room, open office or online

How the hour is spent

Warm-up Postures Relax Pranayama Meditate Q&A
  1. 2′
    Opening

    AUM chanting, Shanthi Mantra (Om Saha Navavatu)

  2. 5′
    Sukshma Vyayama

    Hands in/out breathing, neck rotations, trunk twisting, shoulder rotations

  3. 3′
    Standing: Trikonasana

    Triangle Pose with normal breathing

  4. 3′
    Sitting: Vakrasana

    Twisted Pose with abdominal awareness

  5. 3′
    Prone: Makarasana

    Crocodile Pose with diaphragmatic breathing

  6. 3′
    Supine: Pavanamuktasana

    Wind-Relieving Pose

  7. 10′
    Relaxation

    Guided Deep / Quick / Instant Relaxation (DRT, QRT, IRT)

  8. 10′
    Pranayama

    Bhastrika (modified for high blood pressure), Nadi Shuddhi

  9. 6′
    Bhramari

    Humming "MMM" sound resonance

  10. 10′
    Meditation

    MSRT, Sankalpa ("I am peaceful, I am healthy"), closing Shanthi Patha

  11. 5′
    Integration

    Laughter yoga (Simha Garjana) and Q&A

For event organisers: space, attire & setup
  • Space: conference room, open office area, or a virtual broadcast. Office chairs or yoga mats.
  • Attire: comfortable work clothes are fine. Practise on an empty stomach or at least 2 hours after a light meal.
  • Inclusive: every practice has easier options for joint sensitivity and all fitness levels.
  • Audio: for hybrid or large groups, arrange a clear microphone for guided relaxation and breathwork.
